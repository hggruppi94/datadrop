About DataDrop

DataDrop is a free, open-source tool that turns any CSV file into an interactive dashboard — instantly, in your browser.

No coding required. No sign-up. No server. Your data never leaves your machine.

Features

  • Auto-detect column types — numbers, dates, and text are identified automatically
  • Smart chart suggestions — generates bar, line, pie, and scatter charts based on your data shape
  • Interactive filters — filter by any text column, search across all data
  • Sortable table — click any column header to sort ascending/descending
  • Column insights — min, max, average, unique counts at a glance
  • Export — download your filtered data as CSV
  • 100% private — everything runs in your browser, zero data transmitted
